# Enterprise Billing and Refund Policy

## Billing cycle
Enterprise contracts are billed either annually in advance or net-30 monthly via automated bank transfer (ACH / SEPA) or corporate credit card. Invoices are generated on the 1st of each calendar month with payment terms specified on the master service agreement.

## Refund eligibility
- Annual prepaid commitments: Eligible for prorated refund within the first 30 days of contract execution if service capability fails to meet technical criteria documented in the statement of work.
- Add-on compute capacity: Unused pre-purchased execution credits expire at the end of each annual term and are non-refundable.
- Overage charges: Calculated based on peak volume exceeding provisioned limits at the contracted tier rates.

## Disputed charges
Customers disputing any line item must notify Accounts Receivable within 15 days of invoice dispatch. Uncontested amounts must be settled within the normal payment window while disputed items are reviewed by finance.
